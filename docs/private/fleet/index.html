How the list is built

FMCSA Motor Carrier Census (Aug 2026 pull, 10+ power units, 7-county metro) plus a web-researched light-duty list. Cars and vans only: a carrier qualifies when it reports at least one car or van and those are the majority of its fleet. Truck and tractor fleets, and carriers with no vehicle breakdown, are left out. Cars and vans only. A carrier qualifies when it reports at least one car or van and those are the majority of its fleet; truck and tractor fleets, and carriers with no vehicle breakdown, are left out. Each prospect is then scored 0 to 100: private fleet 40, passenger fleet 25, for-hire carrier 10; all cars and vans 25, mostly cars and vans 15; 10 to 100 of them 15; a fleet-heavy category (courier, landscaping, food, contractor, medical, utility) 10; a phone on file 5; within 2 miles of the store 10. Fleets whose reported size looks like a data error lose 30. Companies from the researched light-duty list start at 75.

Coverage: the federal census only records vehicles over 10,000 lbs, so most pest-control cars and plumber vans never appear in it, and the pull covers Illinois' 7 metro counties only. That is why many stores show few or no prospects; a category search of local service businesses near each store is the next source. Matt's existing fleet accounts are not marked; send the list and they will be.

Sources: FMCSA Motor Carrier Census via data.transportation.gov (pulled August 2026), light-duty list researched August 2026, Fleet Leads Google Sheet. Refresh prospects: node scripts/fleet-prospects.mjs in the competitor-map project. Leads refresh daily.
