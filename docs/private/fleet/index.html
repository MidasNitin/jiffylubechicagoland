Fleet

Who to call, and who has already raised a hand. How the list is built

Google Places category search near each store (Oct 2026) plus the FMCSA Motor Carrier Census (Aug 2026 pull, 10+ power units, 7-county metro) and a web-researched light-duty list. Cars and vans only. A carrier qualifies when it reports at least one car or van and those are the majority of its fleet; truck and tractor fleets, and carriers with no vehicle breakdown, are left out. Each prospect is then scored 0 to 100: private fleet 40, passenger fleet 25, for-hire carrier 10; all cars and vans 25, mostly cars and vans 15; 10 to 100 of them 15; a fleet-heavy category (courier, landscaping, food, contractor, medical, utility) 10; a phone on file 5; within 2 miles of the store 10. Fleets whose reported size looks like a data error lose 30. Companies from the researched light-duty list start at 75. Local service businesses from the Google Maps search start at 45, plus up to 10 for the trade (plumbing, HVAC, pest control and couriers rank highest), up to 20 for review count (200 or more reviews 20, 50 to 199 reviews 14, 10 to 49 reviews 8), 5 for a phone, 3 for a website and 3 for a 4.5 rating or better.

The summary tiles count each company once. The stores table counts a company under every store within reach, so one plumber can appear on three stores' lists and those columns add up to more than the tiles.

Coverage: the federal census only records vehicles over 10,000 lbs, so most pest-control cars and plumber vans never appear in it, and the pull covers Illinois' 7 metro counties only. That is why the Google Maps category search was added: it finds the plumbers, HVAC crews, pest-control routes and couriers that actually run cars and vans near each store. Review count is only a rough size signal, so a quick call still needs to confirm the fleet. Matt's existing fleet accounts are not marked; send the list and they will be.

Sources: Google Places category search (October 2026), FMCSA Motor Carrier Census via data.transportation.gov (pulled August 2026), light-duty list researched August 2026, Fleet Leads Google Sheet. Refresh prospects: node scripts/fleet-places.mjs then node scripts/fleet-prospects.mjs in the competitor-map project. Leads refresh daily.
