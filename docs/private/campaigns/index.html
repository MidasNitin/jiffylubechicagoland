Campaigns

Website traffic and conversions from Google Analytics for the last 90 days, through yesterday. Pulled 2026-10-21 13:30 UTC. Conversions are the five key events: get coupon, copy code, add to wallet, call, directions. Conversion rate is the share of sessions with at least one key event (one visit that taps Get coupon, adds to wallet and gets directions counts once, not three times). Only visits from Illinois, Indiana, Wisconsin count; Google Ads spend, clicks and impressions are whole, since cost cannot be split by state. Excluded so far: 214 visits and 85 key events from other states, mostly our own testing (California 23, Colorado 5, Iowa 5, Texas 8).

What people searched, and what to do about it

Every search term that triggered an ad in the whole pull period, with a suggested action. Add as exact: converted and is not yet a keyword. Consider negative: spent with no conversion, or looks like research, dealer, equipment or job intent. Lower bid: an existing keyword spending without a conversion yet. Working: converting on a keyword we already have. Watch: too little data. Brand, oil-change and competitor terms are never flagged negative. Suggestions are rules, not decisions; the ad group is known only for terms that produced a visit.
